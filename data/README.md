# Released data

All files are comma-separated (CSV) with a header row and full floating-point precision. They are the
saved results of the study; none has been recomputed for this release. Time is given in hours from
00:00 at the start of each 15-min interval (`t_h` = 0.00, 0.25, ..., 23.75; 96 intervals per day).

**Sign conventions.** `P_B > 0`: BESS discharge, `P_B < 0`: BESS charge. `P_MV0 > 0`: import from the
MV grid. Reserves and envelope limits are reported as non-negative magnitudes for each direction.

**Strategies.** B0: no BESS; B1: conventional SOC-based BESS; B2: degradation-aware BESS; DFM: proposed
dynamic flexibility management. Ablation: M0 = DFM; M1 without the health/degradation limitation;
M2 without the uncertainty reserve; M3 without the network envelope.

**Provenance.** *Direct output*: written by the closed-loop simulation or the network model for each
interval. *Model input*: profile or limit computed by the study and supplied to the EMS. *Processed*:
aggregated from direct outputs (per trial or across trials). *Validation*: result of a verification test.

## Column dictionary

### `results_profiles.csv`
`t_mid_h` interval midpoint [h] at which the profiles are evaluated; `P_ac_kW`, `Q_ac_kvar` total AC
demand; `P_dc_kW` total DC demand; `P_pv_kW`, `P_wt_kW` available PV and wind power [kW];
`price_EUR_kWh` energy price.

### `results_B0_renewables.csv`
`alpha` uniform curtailment fraction of the DC-side renewable generation required to remove
renewable-driven constraint violations; `P_curt_kW` curtailed power; `e_max_nocurt`, `bind_nocurt`
largest normalized constraint exceedance and its constraint group before curtailment; `e_max` after
curtailment; `P_MV0_kW` grid import; `loss_kW` network losses.

### `precompute_network_forecast.csv`
`P_grid0`, `loss0` grid import and losses with `P_B = 0` [kW]; `alpha0`, `C0` required curtailment
fraction and power [kW]; `g_dis`, `g_ch` sensitivity of the required curtailment to BESS discharge and
charge [kW/kW]; `P_net_dis`, `P_net_ch` network-deliverable discharge and charge power [kW] (largest
power that creates or aggravates no constraint violation, 0.25-kW bisection tolerance); `net_bind_dis`,
`net_bind_ch` binding constraint group (`S_vsc` converter apparent power, `I_feeder` feeder-head
current, `none` = not network-limited); `P_res_avail` available renewable power [kW].

### `log_nominal_<strategy>.csv`, `log_trial0_DFM.csv` (per interval)

| Column(s) | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `soc`, `soc_next` | SOC at the start and end of the interval | - |
| `soh`, `F`, `E_use` | State of health, linearized degradation, usable energy (SOH x rated energy) | -, -, kWh |
| `ref_*` | Reference DFM envelope evaluated at the current battery state (logged for every strategy) | kW |
| `st_*` | Envelope of the strategy itself (DFM-type strategies only) | kW |
| `*_P_en_*`, `*_P_deg_*`, `*_P_net_*` | Energy/health, degradation and network limits (discharge `dis`, charge `ch`) | kW |
| `*_P_res_*`, `*_P_flex_*`, `*_P_safe_*` | Uncertainty reserve, physical envelope (minimum of the three limits), safe envelope (physical envelope minus reserve) | kW |
| `*_lim_dis`, `*_lim_ch` | Active limiter: `en`, `deg`, `net`, or `rated` when no limit is below the rated power | - |
| `D_allow`, `D_max_cyc`, `D_used_cyc` | Remaining, daily maximum and used cycle-degradation allowance | SOH units |
| `P_sched` | BESS power scheduled by the EMS | kW |
| `eps_net` | Realized DC-subsystem net forecast error (0 on the nominal day) | kW |
| `P_B`, `P_dis`, `P_ch` | Executed BESS power and its discharge/charge parts | kW |
| `dev` | Forecast-error compensation that the BESS could not provide (`P_sched + eps_net - P_B`) | kW |
| `rt_net_limited` | Real-time correction reduced to avoid creating or aggravating a network violation | bool |
| `t_solve`, `ems_ok`, `ems_status` | EMS solution time, success flag, solver status | s, -, - |
| `dF_cyc`, `dF_cal`, `dD`, `E_bess_loss`, `soc_clip` | Cycle and calendar degradation increments, SOH decrease, BESS conversion loss, numerical SOC clipping | -, -, -, kWh, - |
| `alpha`, `P_curt`, `P_res_avail_real` | Curtailment fraction, curtailed power, realized available renewable power | -, kW, kW |
| `P_MV0`, `loss`, `price`, `P_ac`, `P_dc` | Grid import, network losses, price, realized AC and DC demand | kW, kW, EUR/kWh, kW, kW |
| `V_min`, `V_max` | Network-wide minimum and maximum AC phase-to-neutral voltage | V |
| `Vdc_min`, `Vdc_max` | Network-wide minimum and maximum DC voltage | V |
| `I_R01`, `I_I01`, `I_C01` | Largest phase current at the residential, industrial and commercial feeder heads (0.4-kV side) | A |
| `I_dc_max`, `S_H01`, `util_vsc_max` | Largest DC line current; apparent power of the I01-H01 converter; largest converter apparent power over rating | A, kVA, - |
| `e_max`, `e_<group>` | Largest normalized constraint exceedance (> 0 = violation), overall and per constraint group | - |
| `pf_ok` | Network solution converged | bool |

The AC voltages are normalized by 231 V and the DC voltages by 800 V in the figures. Feeder loading
is the phase current divided by the feeder-head current limit of the study (724.6 / 231.9 / 434.8 A (R01/I01/C01); Industrial (I01): 160-kVA current basis).

### Per-trial and summary files
Per-trial files (`*_trials.csv`) contain one row per strategy (or configuration) and realization
(`trial` = -1 for the nominal day). Main columns: `E_grid_kWh`, `cost_EUR` grid energy and cost;
`E_grid_corr_kWh`, `cost_corr_EUR` the same, corrected by the energy needed to restore the initial SOC
(valued at the valley price); `E_curt_kWh`, `eta_RES` curtailment and renewable utilization;
`E_loss_kWh` network losses; `dSOH_rainflow`, `dSOH_online` SOH consumption by rainflow counting and by
the online estimator; `N_EFC` equivalent full cycles; `E_dev_kWh` uncovered imbalance;
`n_viol_intervals` intervals with a violation; `max_exceedance` largest normalized exceedance;
`t_solve_*_ms` EMS solution times; `mean_P_*_kW` mean envelope widths; `share_lim_*` share of intervals
per active limiter; `eps_risk`, `H_f_h`, `soh_0`, `N_ref` design parameters of the run; `tag`, `config`
experiment identifiers. Summary files report `nom_*` (nominal day), `mc_mean_*` and `mc_P95_*` (100
realizations), `mc_P_viol_trials` (share of realizations with a violation) and `rel_mc_*_pct` (relative
change of the Monte Carlo mean with respect to M0).
